New York City's Traffic Problem May Come From More Than Just Gridlock

For most driving through the city, especially in Manhattan, gridlock is the cause of so many headaches. While many think that the amount of traffic should be a crime, it can't really be reported to the police. For these kinds of annoyances and more minor crimes, New York City has a special line to report them, which then stores the data for public access. Under the traffic section, there are five categories of report: speeding, stoplight violations, drag racing, truck route violations, and congestion. With how many people complain about NYC gridlocks, one would assume that would be the highest reported violation. Strangely enough, however, one type of violation in only one borough far exceeds any other.
